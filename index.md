I received my PhD in 
<a href="https://steinhardt.nyu.edu/departments/communicative-sciences-and-disorders" target = "_blank">Communicative Sciences and Disorders</a> from New York University. I study linguistic accommodation in intergroup contexts, examining how social identities and attitudes toward those identities shape the ways in which interlocutors linguistically accommodate one another in interactions; and how such accommodations are perceived by the listeners. My work focuses especially on contexts involving autistic individuals and uses psycholinguistic methods to study these dynamics.

Other primary areas of interest include: 
* how parent-child interactions support language learning
* pragmatic development of blind and newly-sighted children
* how optional linguistic markers are used and acquired

Current mentor-collaborators: <a href="https://steinhardt.nyu.edu/people/sudha-arunachalam" target = "_blank">Dr. Sudha Arunachalam (NYU)</a>, <a href="https://psychology.as.virginia.edu/people/vikram-jaswal" target = "_blank">Dr. Vikram Jaswal (University of Virginia)</a> and <a href="https://www.mpi.nl/people/rubio-fernandez-paula" target = "_blank">Dr. Paula Rubio-Fernández (Max Planck Institute for Psycholinguistics)</a>.
