I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at **F**acial **A**ffective and **C**ommunicative **E**xpressions (FACE) Lab at Emerson College, Boston.

I received my PhD in 
<a href="https://steinhardt.nyu.edu/departments/communicative-sciences-and-disorders" target = "_blank">Communicative Sciences and Disorders</a> from New York University. I studied linguistic accommodation in interactions with autistic individuals, examining how interlocutors linguistically accommodate them; and how such accommodations impact autistic listeners' comprehension and perception of the speaker.

Other areas of interest include: 
* how parent-child interactions support language learning
* pragmatic development of blind and newly-sighted children
* how optional linguistic markers are used and acquired

Current mentor-collaborators: <a href="https://emerson.edu/faculty/ruth-grossman/" target = "_blank">Dr. Ruth Grossman (Emerson College)</a>, <a href="https://steinhardt.nyu.edu/people/sudha-arunachalam" target = "_blank">Dr. Sudha Arunachalam (NYU)</a>, <a href="https://psychology.as.virginia.edu/people/vikram-jaswal" target = "_blank">Dr. Vikram Jaswal (University of Virginia)</a> and <a href="https://www.mpi.nl/people/rubio-fernandez-paula" target = "_blank">Dr. Paula Rubio-Fernández (Max Planck Institute for Psycholinguistics)</a>.
